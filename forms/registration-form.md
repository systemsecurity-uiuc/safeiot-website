## Form settings

- Collect email addresses: **Responder input** — this is a setting, not a question. Settings tab → Responses → Collect email addresses.
  Forms adds the email box at the top of the form by itself; do not create an email question. "Responder input" is chosen over "Verified" so that responders do not need a Google account.
- Restrict to users in a domain: **off**
- Limit to 1 response: **off**
- Allow response editing: **on**
- Send responders a copy of their response: **on (Always)**
- File upload questions: **none**
- Responses: link to a Google Sheet — useful for mail-merging the webinar links and reminders.
  Keep this sheet internal: it holds registrants' personal data and the judges have no need for it.
- Closing date: none — keep open until the submission deadline

- Publishing: the form is a draft until you click **Publish**. In the Publish dialog set the audience to **Anyone with the link**.
  The link to put on the website is the **responder link** (`https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/<FORM_ID>/viewform`) — never the `/edit` URL from your address bar.
  You can keep editing after publishing; the responder link stays the same and edits go live immediately.
  Publish this form as soon as it is ready — registration should be open now.
